MODULAR MODERN HOUSE PACK – UNITY SETUP

Ready-to-use parts:
Assets/Models/Buildings/ModularModernHousePack/Prefabs

The folders contain separate prefabs for:
- Walls and wall/window combinations
- Windows
- Doors
- Flat and sloped roofs
- Balcony, railing, lamps, hedge, bike stand and stone path

The parts use a 2.5 m / 5 m modular grid. Place them with Unity's grid snapping
or hold V for vertex snapping. Each prefab is grounded at y=0, has a collider,
uses optimized 2K PBR textures and is marked static for batching and light probes.
