READY-TO-USE PREFABS

After Unity finishes importing, drag these files into the scene:

SmallRedGasStation/Prefabs/Small Red Gas Station.prefab
ConvenienceStore/Prefabs/Convenience Store.prefab

The prefabs are centered, grounded and use local material copies. Source models and
textures remain in their respective Source and Textures folders.

If a prefab is missing, use:
Tools > CYDOY > Buildings > Rebuild Imported Building Prefabs
